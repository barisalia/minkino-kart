1. SCREEN RECORDING
The attached screen recording was captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts with launching the app and shows the typical flow: age selection, main menu, activities, games, books and cartoons. The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content shared with others, and no paid content in this version (everything is free, there are no in-app purchases).

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Minkino is a Turkish-language, fully voiced learning and play app for preschool children aged 3-6. Two characters, Mino (a cat) and Kino (a dog), guide the child with spoken instructions, so children who cannot read yet can play on their own. It helps with early skills: counting, colors, shapes, matching, drawing, listening and daily routines (getting ready for school). There are no ads and wrong answers are never punished.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP
No login, account or sample files are needed. Open the app, pick the child's age (3, 4, 5 or 6) and tap any card in the main menu: Activities (Okula Hazırlanalım, Bil Bakalım, Çiz Canlansın, Sesli Maceralar), Games, Books (read-along picture books) and Cartoons. Settings and external links are behind a parental gate (a simple question for adults).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- YouTube: the Cartoons section plays the developer's own videos from our YouTube channel (MinkinoKids) using the youtube-nocookie.com embedded player.
- Everything else (pictures, voice recordings, games) is bundled inside the app and works offline. There are no ad, login, payment or AI services in this version.
- Microphone (optional): some activities detect the child blowing or clapping in real time on the device. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Without permission, every activity works by tapping.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. Its content and voice are in Turkish.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
All characters, drawings, stories, voices and videos are our own original work. The app is not in a regulated industry.
